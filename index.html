Notes

Everything runs in your browser; no video leaves this machine. Training is few-shot: each example is stored as a feature vector and new frames are classified by their 10 nearest stored examples, so adding a word needs no retraining. Speech output and recognition use the browser's built-in voices — Chrome is required for recognition. For Hindi or Kannada output, add words in that script.
